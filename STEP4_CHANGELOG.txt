Redgrin Listener — Step 4

- Fixed a serious stun bug: the stun item previously disabled the Listener AI
  without ever restoring it, which could leave him permanently frozen.
- Added a timed stun method to RedgrinListenerEntity.
- The stun now lasts 100 ticks (5 seconds at 20 ticks per second), then the
  Listener's AI is re-enabled.
- Updated the item's tooltip and comments so they accurately describe its
  current behaviour.

Limitations:
- The item currently activates a stun pulse around the clicked block. It is
  not yet a persistent placed trap that waits for the Listener to walk over it.
- This remains source code, not a compiled or in-game-tested JAR.
